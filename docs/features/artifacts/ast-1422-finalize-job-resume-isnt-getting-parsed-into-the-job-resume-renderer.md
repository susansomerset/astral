### Description

## As-is

`finalize_job_resume` succeeded for this job (`e2a208a4-05e8-4470-b9ac-01db0a2bc7ff`). `job_data.artifacts.job_resume` holds the RESPONSE `agent_data_id` (pin only). That `agent_data` row still has the full `agent_payload.resume` (name, title, tagline, contact, summary, highlights, experience job-array). Nothing on the job record is an editable resume blob — no `artifacts.resume_content`. `artifacts.deviations` *was* copied onto the job. Recommended-job Job Resume fields are blank; Preview/Print shows basic contact and no title. `agent_data` itself was not written after the hop (correct).

## To-be

On a successful `finalize_job_resume`, the parsed resume is **copied** from that RESPONSE onto the job record as an editable blob. The `agent_data_id` pin stays on the job. Later edits write the job blob only. The `agent_data` table is never edited.

## Proposed steps

1. After the pin write, copy the unwrapped `agent_payload.resume` onto the job as the editable blob (the `resume_content` slot AST-551/552 used — a sibling of the pin, not a dict stuffed into `job_resume` that would clobber the id).
2. Job Resume editor and Preview/Print read that job blob first; the pin is provenance / fallback, not the edit target.
3. Save paths write the job blob only. Do not replace `artifacts.job_resume` with a dict (AST-1100 allowed that; this ticket should not).
4. Leave `agent_data` rows immutable.
