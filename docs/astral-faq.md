## Question 1: how does this work?
The intake process answers three deep questions: what kind of work do you really want to do, what kind of companies would be a good place for you to work, what kind of jobs would be right in your wheelhouse.

Then we have a platform that both actively seeks jobs for you from well-suited companies, and receives jobs from you, yourself, as you are looking independently, or even a forwarded email from a recruiter.

The job is sized up to determine if you could DO the job, if you could GET the job, and if you would LIKE the job.  

At your direction it then zshuzshes your base resume without fabrication (which works because we have already established you can reasonable get and do the job), and proposes an insightful cover letter draft that is invariably not terribly good because it is drafted by AI, but gives you a spring board to revise or rewrite.  All drafted content is reviewed and revised by you.  The goal is to unwedge you, not impersonate you.

Application submissions are for you to do, but we connect you with the jobs that are genuinely worthy of your attention.

## Question 2: why would we aggressively filter when we might need to find work as soon as possible?
The job market is a numbers game at this point.  Spray and pray is a bitter reality.  However, when you do get a hit with that method, you run the risk of wasting time with conversations about jobs that are a poor fit for you, and as such, will probably not be awarded to you in favor of other better-suited candidates.

It is intended to dramatically increase the chances that a hit will lead to a hire.  Therefore, “fewer” applications does not result in lower likelihood of hire.

We also, particularly for FTE roles, build a curated yet robust roster of companies (from hundreds to thousands) to watch regularly over time, so that when the right job appears, you hear all about it on day 1.  That wider net means a greater likelihood of a suitable role coming up so you can apply in a timely manner.

## Question 3: do you use existing job boards like LinkedIn and Indeed as a source?
No, not directly.  We had considered using saved searches on job aggregator boards as sources for candidate jobs, but by nature they want you to use their platform the find jobs, and many job search sites are bot-blocked, which is a nonstarter for this model.

INSTEAD, we can do things like use text from job description pages you have found, or emails from recruiters, and perform our analysis based on that content.  It may be a little janky, but perhaps we can create a browser extension that could perform this for our candidates at some point.  (“To the backlog!”)

## Question 4: does this replace the grueling work of job hunting?
Replace? No. Make more efficient and less hassle? Absolutely.

As you find jobs in your search, Astral analysis will give you the straight dope about the role and your chances, and generate the materials to apply for the job with meaningful content in minutes.

## Question 5: why won't you just apply for me?
Put simply: applying on your behalf would betray the mission of the platform.  Your job, your application, the real you. Ideally, the jobs we recommend will be an actual pleasure to apply for.

## Question 6: how do I get started?
We set up a private channel for you right here on this slack, and we talk about your work history, your priorities, dealbreakers, etc., with the goal of developing a structured profile “base” resume that will inform a set of criteria by which we will assess each job description.

We go at your pace, to your level of comfort, and we draft all the materials for you.

All you need to do is talk with us and share some pre-existing files (like one or more resumes, one or more cover letters you liked, and any relevant professional profile content (e.g. LinkedIn, Upwork, etc.).
